## Brand & Style

This design system revitalizes the identity of Club Social y Deportivo Los Cooperarios de Quilmes Oeste. It translates the heritage of a traditional Argentine neighborhood social and athletic institution into an authoritative, agile, and contemporary digital ecosystem. The aesthetic balances social warmth and democratic community governance with the rigorous, high-performance vitality of an athletic club.

The aesthetic philosophy fuses **Modern Corporate Structure** with **Athletic Dynamism**:
- **Character & Temperament:** Grounded, communal, dignified, and vigorous. Visuals avoid playful novelty in favor of structured order, institutional pride, and clear legibility.
- **Target Audience:** Multi-generational club partners, athletes, parents, administration staff, and community members. The experience must deliver immediate operational trust for administrative and membership tasks (dues, tournament schedules, facility bookings, institutional notices) while retaining athletic energy.
- **Atmospheric Expression:** Bright, structured canvases grounded in rich crimson tones, crisp graphite typographic contrast, purposeful warmth in neutral planes, and deliberate functional status signifiers.

## Colors

The color system is anchored by the historical athletic crimson of the club, stabilized by deep graphite neutrals and calibrated feedback accents:

- **Primary (`#B91C1C` / Athletic Crimson):** Represents the core institutional identity, passion, and competitive spirit. Used for key actions, brand banners, primary navigation triggers, and prominent identity markers. An interactive hover variant steps into `#991B1B`.
- **Secondary (`#1F2937` / Deep Graphite):** Provides structural weight and institutional authority. Applied to primary headings, high-priority navigation bars, deep containers, and data-dense athletic tables.
- **Tertiary (`#F9FAFB` to `#F3F4F6` / Warm White & Light Gray):** Subtle, warm-tinted clean backdrops that prevent sterile starkness, offering a warm community-oriented surface.
- **Neutral (`#4B5563` / Slate Gray):** Handles secondary body copy, passive iconography, structural dividers (`#E5E7EB`), and muted metadata.
- **Functional States:**
  - **Success / Cuota al Día (`#15803D` / Deep Field Green):** Conveys validated membership status, processed payments, and match victories.
  - **Warning / En Revisión (`#D97706` / Amber Gold):** Alerts users to pending dues, schedule shifts, or capacity thresholds.
  - **Critical / Mora (`#DC2626`):** Identifies urgent disciplinary notifications, administrative debts, or cancellations.

## Typography

The type system relies exclusively on **Inter**, exploiting its structural versatility, neutral geometry, and high legibility across athletic scoreboards, digital member carnets, and dense administrative tables.

- **Headlines (`headline-xl`, `headline-lg`, `headline-md`):** Set with tightly tracked letterforms (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) and heavy weights (700 to 800) to project athletic urgency, institutional presence, and confidence.
- **Body (`body-lg`, `body-md`, `body-sm`):** Calibrated for effortless scanning of official communiqués, bylaws, fixture listings, and payment breakdowns.
- **Labels (`label-lg`, `label-md`, `label-sm`):** Rendered in medium to bold weights with slight positive tracking for badges, table column headers, match timestamps, category tags (e.g., *Sub-17*, *Veteranos*, *Socio Activo*), and system feedback.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt structural rhythm combined with a responsive, container-centric 12-column grid.

- **Desktop (1024px and up):** 12-column fluid grid, `2rem` (32px) margins, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, capped at a maximum width of `1280px` for standard portal dashboards and editorial content.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` (24px) margins and gutters. Side-by-side components stack logically (e.g., membership summary moves above monthly calendar).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` (16px) margins and gutters. Athletic tables reflow into unified swipeable cards, and sticky primary actions lock to the base of the viewport.
- **Component Rhythms:** Internal padding scales strictly from `space-sm` (8px) for badges and compact cells, to `space-md` (16px) for standard field containers, and `space-lg` (24px) for cards and modular panels.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve an athletic, functional, and reliable identity, visual depth is achieved through **tonal stratification combined with subtle, high-precision ambient shadows** rather than heavy skeuomorphism or aggressive blur layers.

- **Base Canvas:** `#F9FAFB` (Subtle off-white warm neutral), providing clean grounding.
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards, Modules, Table Containers):** Pure `#FFFFFF` resting on top of the base. Delimited by a crisp, low-contrast border (`#E5E7EB`, 1px solid) and an ultra-diffused ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.05), 0 1px 2px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Carnets, Popovers, Dropdowns, Match Result Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an elevated ambient drop: `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Modals, Urgent Notifications, Emergency Banners):** Crisp elevated container with a graphite-tinted overlay (`rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.6)`), focusing user attention strictly on transactional tasks like payment confirmation or club member registration.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a standard curvature of **12px (`0.75rem` / `rounded-lg`)** across all core containers, balancing structural institutional discipline with welcoming, human-centric modernity.

- **Core Elements (12px / 0.75rem):** Standard buttons, text inputs, metric display cards, match banners, and member credential cards use a continuous 12px corner radius.
- **Micro Elements (6px - 8px / 0.375rem - 0.5rem):** Form checkboxes, radio anchors, tooltips, and compact data tags scale down proportionally to avoid visual bulbousness.
- **Pill Exceptions (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for status indicators (e.g., *Cuota al Día*, *Suspendido*, *En Torneo*), sport category chips, and live score tags.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action:** Solid Crimson (`#B91C1C`) fill, pure white text, 12px border radius, medium weight (`label-lg`), height 44px (touch-compliant). Hover shifts to `#991B1B` with smooth 150ms transition. Active state scales subtly (98%).
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with 1.5px solid Graphite (`#1F2937`) border, `#1F2937` typography. Hover transitions to `#F3F4F6`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No border, crimson text, faint crimson wash (`#FEF2F2`) on hover. Used for auxiliary operations like "Ver reglamento" or "Descargar comprobante".

### Digital Member Carnet (Carnet de Socio Digital)
- Designed to replicate physical credential trust: crisp white card bounded by a 1px border (`#E5E7EB`) and an athletic crimson top-accent stripe (6px).
- Displays the member's photo (12px rounded), member number in bold mono-spaced Inter, full legal name, current category, and an unmistakable status pill (*Cuota al Día* in `#15803D` with light green tint `#DCFCE7`). Incorporates a validated QR verification module for facility turnstiles.

### Status Chips & Badges
- Compact indicators (height 24px-28px) with full pill curvature (`rounded-full`), uppercase `label-sm` tracking.
- **Success:** `#15803D` text on `#DCFCE7` background.
- **Pending/Warning:** `#D97706` text on `#FEF3C7` background.
- **Overdue/Critical:** `#B91C1C` text on `#FEE2E2` background.

### Input Fields & Form Elements
- Inputs feature a crisp 12px radius, 44px base height, `#FFFFFF` background, and `#D1D5DB` border.
- **Focus State:** 2px ring in Crimson (`#B91C1C`) with a soft matching ambient glow, instantly grounding accessibility.
- Checkboxes and radios adopt a 6px rounded corner and circular geometry respectively, adopting `#B91C1C` when active.

### Fixtures, Tables & Result Cards
- Compact, dense layout with subtle zebra striping (`#F9FAFB` alternating with `#FFFFFF`). Headers in `#1F2937` with bold, uppercase `label-md` labeling.
- Match score displays use high-contrast numerical tracking in Graphite with Crimson highlight accents on winning squads.
